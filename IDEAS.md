## Chord analyzer

Three possible versions, easiest first:

1. **Knows your guitar:** from the copedent, shows the chords available at each fret with which pedals and levers. Doesn't need to listen.
2. **Names what you play:** you play a chord and it names it. Knowing the copedent narrows down the possibilities.
3. **Checks each note in a chord:** tells you how in tune each note is. Overlapping overtones make this hard, so it would need testing before promising anything.
